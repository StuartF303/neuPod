    Bench practice and test order¶

    How the Pico 2 W testbed is driven from the PC, and the agreed order of the next bench tests.

    Tools and sequence¶

    1. scripts/build.sh rp2350 (add --clean after a CMake option change).
    2. scripts/flash.sh [rp2350] [target] over the Debug Probe (CMSIS-DAP): programs, verifies, reset halt, then resumes core 1 and core 0. A plain OpenOCD program ... reset exit leaves core 1 halted in the bootrom (PC 0xDA) and the audio core never runs; see scripts/README.md.
    3. scripts/console.sh — UART0 at 115200 on the Debug Probe.

    Driving the UI without the wheel¶

    The wheel is not fitted (the host → wheel init packet is still unknown, see Click-wheel protocol). UART keys: w/s scroll, e select, m back, a/d left/right, p play/pause, l toggles Hold (the rig has no Power key, ADR-0020). A burst of keys is queued (64 events); if the UI ever could not keep up the 10 s statistics print input: WARNING n events dropped.

    • Console lines end in \r; strip it before matching a line in a script.
    • Confirm the logged label of the highlighted row before sending Select (e); never press Select blind on a list row.

    Practices¶

    • Never pkill -f <pattern> from a script: the pattern matches the script's own shell. Kill by PID.
    • Headphones off your head for the first sound from any new audio path; start with the Pack's gain switch low.
    • When a breakout is silent, read the pin state back over SWD before metering (OpenOCD mdw on SIO_IN 0xd0000004 and IO_BANK0 GPIOn_STATUS 0x40028000 + 8n, no halt needed): it separates firmware from wiring in one step.
    • Count Pico pins from pin 1 (GP0, by the USB connector); GND pins 3/8/13/18/23/28/33/38 sit next to signal pins, so an off-by-one jumper often lands on ground.
    • Record measured figures (fps, RAM, heap low-water, underruns) in the page for the feature, with the date.

    Next bench tests (agreed 3 Nov 2026)¶

    1. Audio Pack tone test — 1 kHz on the line-out jack done 3 Nov 2026; still to do: L/R ident, scale, volume steps, phones jack (PAM8908 amp, gain switch) (Audio).
    2. Display fps baseline — re-run test 7 of the display test suite (Display tests) and note the figure as the baseline for the UI-performance work.
    3. MP3 and ALAC playback (files in /Music).
    4. Settings persistence (backlight, click feedback).
    5. Haptic click per list step.
    6. SD recovery: flash mid-playback.
    7. Bluetooth scan with the AirPods in pairing mode (expect audio=yes).
    8. Battery / charging detection (VBUS + CRATE heuristic, Requirements → Power).

    Bluetooth reconnect-on-play (Pico 2 W + AirPods)¶

    For the build with reconnect-on-play (Bluetooth). Pair the AirPods once, set Audio output to Auto (or Bluetooth), keep the console open (scripts/console.sh). Lines to watch: route: playing with no headset stream, waiting (...), bt: reconnecting, bt: connect / bt: connected, bt: a2dp stream open, route: asking the headset to start the stream (n), bt: a2dp streaming, bt: a2dp command rejected by the headset (signal 7, start), bt: a2dp start dropped, route: no stream start after 3 requests, closing and reopening it, and the endings route: ... , paused. Toasts: "Connecting to ...", " connected", "Couldn't connect to - paused".

    1. Idle hang-up, then Play. Play a track, pause, wait for the AirPods to hang up (bt: acl down, reason 0x13, bt: link closed ... (idle); about 45 s). The output stays Bluetooth and nothing connects by itself. Press Play: the toast says "Connecting to ...", the player shows Playing, bt: reconnecting then bt: connected, bt: a2dp stream open, bt: a2dp streaming, sound within a few seconds from where it was paused.
    2. Drop mid-track, Play after the reconnect. Play, walk out of range (or put the AirPods in the closed case with the lid shut for a moment): Headset disconnected and the player pauses. Come back, let Reconnecting... finish (bt: a2dp stream open; playback stays paused). Press Play: bt: a2dp streaming and sound. This is bug 2 of 19 Nov: if it still stays silent, keep the console log from the Play: look for bt: a2dp command rejected ... start, route: asking the headset to start the stream (1..3, about 2 s apart), then route: no stream start after 3 requests, closing and reopening it; Play must either stream or end in "Couldn't connect to ... - paused", never in silence.
    3. Headset not reachable. Put the AirPods in the closed case (after the 0x13 hang-up), press Play: "Connecting to ..." toast, the player shows Playing, two bt: reconnecting attempts about 20 s + 2 s apart (the whole wait is bounded at 60 s), then "Couldn't connect to ... - paused" and route: could not connect, paused; the player is paused at the same place, the output is still Bluetooth (nothing from the jack), and no further bt: reconnecting follows. Press Play again: it tries again.
    4. Play during "Reconnecting...". Drop the link, and while the headset row / toast says reconnecting press Play: "Connecting to ..." (it joins the running reconnect, no extra bt: reconnecting earlier than the schedule's own), and when the AirPods are back (open the case) the track starts by itself: bt: a2dp streaming.
    5. Pause while waiting. In step 3 (case closed) press Pause within the first seconds: no failure toast, the player stays paused, and no second attempt starts (only the one in flight may finish). Also check Disconnect from Settings > Bluetooth while waiting: the player pauses with "Headset not connected" and Auto returns to the wired output.
    6. Auto with the output wired. Boot with the AirPods in the case so the boot retries give up (or Disconnect them): the output is wired; Play plays wired and no bt: reconnecting appears.

    Record in the page for the feature (with the date) what the log showed for step 2.

    Radio priority check (Pico 2 W, after 19 Nov 2026)¶

    The CYW43 worker (BTstack) now runs at priority 6, above storage (5). Re-run Eels FLAC to the AirPods on a static screen and again on Now Playing. Expect A2DP stalls about 0 per 10 s, catch-up in the 25-34 ms range, bitpool steady at 53, no drops, no disconnects. Before the change: 36 stalls per 10 s, catch-up up to 160 ms, bitpool forced to 45. Also check that the higher worker priority does not starve input (4) or I2C (4): during a Bluetooth scan and during the Wi-Fi download test, expect no wheel resync/recovery messages and no increase in the I2C timeout counter.

    Bluetooth walk (Y2, bitpool policy, after 19 Nov 2026)¶

    The bitpool now steps down at the first stall and climbs back only after 30 s clean (doubling to 240 s after a quick fall; Bluetooth). Repeat the 19 Nov walk: Y2 in the left front pocket, AirPods Pro, the same route and music, the HCI trace on (NEUPOD_BT_HCI_DUMP to /opt/neupod-logs) and the log kept. Compare against the 19 Nov walk:

    1. Per 10 s bt: link: line in the bad patches: stalls and drops (19 Nov: e.g. stalls 2, catchup 160 ms, drops 1516 ms). Expect drops far less often and smaller; catchup mostly under 90 ms.
    2. bt: a2dp: bitpool ... lines: expect a fall to 35 at the start of a bad patch (53 -> 35, or 53 -> 45 -> 35 within about 50 ms), no rise within 30 s of a fall, and far fewer changes per hour than 19 Nov (it went 35 -> 45 after 60 to 90 s and 45 -> 53 soon after, then fell again). Note the time spent at 35 and whether it ever gets back to 53 on a good stretch.
    3. Listen: does 35 sound acceptable compared with the scratchy, glitchy audio of 19 Nov? Note any audible click at a bitpool change.
    4. Stream start (Play after a pause, and after a reconnect): no bitpool change in the first second unless the link is bad.

    Sleep (Y2)¶

    Steps for the Y2 after flashing a build with ADR-0021. Unplug USB first (a plugged-in Y2 reports external power and never sleeps); watch the log over the console or /opt/neupod-logs. Expect power: sleep available (suspend to RAM, ...) at start; if it says the kernel offers no suspend or there is no alarm device, the player will power off when idle instead (that is the fallback working).

    1. Settings > Display > Sleep after is 30 min by default; set 10 min for the test. Pause music (or leave it stopped) and do nothing. At 10 min: bt: controller off for sleep, ui: backlight off 0%, ui: hold on, power: going to sleep; the USB link drops. Press Power: the screen comes back dark-locked then lights (ui: backlight wake), the log (on the card or after reconnecting) shows power: woke after N ms asleep (key); the press does nothing else. Check the picture and the backlight are right, the time slept (N) is about the 10 minutes, and that it did not go back to sleep by itself (power: woke once, no second going to sleep until another 10 min).
    2. With a headset paired and connected before the sleep: after the wake bt: controller on after sleep, bt: reconnecting, and " connected" within a minute.
    3. Wake with the wheel and with a ring button: same result (the key is not delivered).
    4. Music playing for longer than the Sleep after time: it must not sleep; 10 min after it ends (or is paused) it does.
    5. Charger in while asleep (bonus): the wake reason is external power if the hardware wakes on it. Charger in while awake: it never sleeps.
    6. Kill switch: NEUPOD_SUSPEND=0 in /etc/neupod-fw.env, restart the firmware: the start-up line says sleep is switched off and, after the idle time, power: idle for the sleep time, powering off.
    7. Measure the current in suspend if you can (the point of the feature), and note how long the sleep and the wake take.
    8. 24 h limit (optional, long): leave it asleep for a day. It should be off by then (not suspended), and the next power-up should come back at the saved position.
    9. After a wake, play a file and browse a folder: no SD I/O errors in the log (the card must be re-detected across the suspend), and the player carries on.
    10. Kill the firmware while it is asleep or just requesting sleep (kill by PID only), let the wrapper restart it: the new start writes on and the screen must be lit (the panel not left early-suspended).
    11. Timer wake: set NEUPOD_SLEEP_MAX_S=120 in /etc/neupod-fw.env, restart, let it sleep: after 2 minutes it must wake with (timer) and power off. Check both clocks in the log: the line woke after N ms asleep (CLOCK_BOOTTIME against CLOCK_MONOTONIC) and that the timer verdict also holds if N is 0 (the real-time clock fallback); note which of the two decided.
    12. Inspect what a wheel touch produces as the wake (the log line says key; check the key is not delivered and nothing opens), and what Power does.
    13. Charger plug-in while asleep: does the hardware wake on it (external power reason)? If not, the unit sleeps on until a key; that is acceptable.

    Hold (Y2)¶

    Steps for the Y2 after flashing a build with ADR-0020 (watch /opt/neupod-logs or the console for the ui: hold, ui: backlight and y2: power held lines):

    1. Settings > Display > Lock hold: set 2 s for steps 1 to 5 (the default is Off, see the Lock hold steps below).
    2. Tap Power (quick): the backlight goes off (ui: backlight off 0%) and ui: hold on follows (Auto-lock is On by default). Turn the wheel or press anything: the screen wakes, shows the pill "Locked · hold Power 2 s to unlock", and nothing else happens (no click, no Play/Pause, no selection moved).
    3. Hold Power: after about 0.4 s a blue line grows under the status bar and the pill says "Keep holding to unlock · 10 s = off"; let go at 1 s: nothing changes. Hold again to 2 s: "Unlocked", one haptic click, the lock icon leaves the status bar, ui: hold off. Keys work again.
    4. Hold Power to 2 s and on: "Locked" appears at 2 s; at 5 s the line turns red and the pill counts "Release to cancel · shutting down in 5"; let go at 7 s: nothing powers off (and nothing is lost). Repeat and hold to 10 s: ui: power-off requested, y2: power held, shutting down, the storage task stops, the player exits with status 77 and the device powers off (the wrapper's poweroff did not happen, restarting must NOT appear).
    5. With music playing and the screen locked: the headset's buttons (play/pause, next, previous) still work; the wheel and the ring buttons do not.
    6. Settings > Display: Screen off after (try 15 s) and Auto-lock Off: the screen goes off with Hold left as it was; the waking press is lost, the next one works.
    7. Charging-only mode (USB with the player "off") is unchanged: Power held 2 s reboots.

    Lock hold setting (2026-11-19, not yet run on hardware)¶

    1. Lock hold Off (the default; Settings > Display): the Auto-lock row is greyed and reads Off, Select on it does nothing. Hold Power 2 s: nothing locks (no "Locked", no click); the pill says "Keep holding to power off" with the line still filling; at 5 s the red countdown starts; let go at 7 s: nothing powers off. Hold to 10 s: the power-off still happens. A tap still turns the screen off and the screen going off (tap or timeout) does not lock.
    2. Set 3 s: Auto-lock is live again (with its old On/Off value). Hold Power: "Locked" at 3 s (not at 2 s), unlock at 3 s; a swallowed key shows "Locked · hold Power 3 s to unlock". With Auto-lock On the screen going off locks.
    3. Set Off while locked: Hold unlocks at once ("Unlocked"). With the console key l (rig) or the slide switch (iPod board) Hold still toggles with Lock hold Off. Power-cycle: the setting is kept.
