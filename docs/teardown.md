# Donor teardown — iPod Classic 6G A1238 (5 Nov 2026)

Photos by Stuart, 5 Nov 2026 (original set: [workphotos report](https://reports.workphotos.com/6aec8184382a8749d5cac1fc)). Scale in the photos is a RasPiO GPIO ruler (cm). Board markings: Apple **820-2437**, "08/10/15 REV:06", © 2008; wheel sub-board **821-0545-A** © 2007; battery flex Foxconn © 2008; cell label GS837630F0953, Li-ion.

## Findings that change the design

1. **The mid-frame is ribbed.** The logic board is screwed to the back of a cast frame whose cross-ribs divide the board area into about six pockets (a 2 × 3 grid, each roughly 25 × 18 mm). Component height under a rib is the board-to-rib gap (≈ 1 mm by eye); only the pockets take tall parts. **Placement must follow the pocket grid** — rib positions and heights are the first targets for the 3D scan.
2. **The battery lives in the back shell**, stuck to the shell with its flex running down to the board's bottom edge. It does not compete with the board for depth: the board side has the frame plus the whole HDD cavity (the foam-wrapped 1.8" disk sits over the board in the front half). Without the HDD there is more depth on the board side than the "~6 mm thin" open-source estimate, and a thicker cell can go in the shell.
3. **Headphone jack + hold switch are one flex** screwed into the top-left corner of the back shell, with a long flex to a small connector at the board's bottom-right. Reusable as-is ([ADR-0018](adr/0018-headphone-jack-reuse.md)).
4. **Connectors on the board**: wide ZIF for the LCD (top-middle), narrow ZIF for the wheel (top-right, ~8–10 conductors), headphone/hold flex connector (bottom-right), battery connector (bottom-left), 40-pin HDD ZIF and the 30-pin dock along the bottom edge. Pitches and pin counts still to be measured.
5. **Wheel assembly 821-0545-A**: capacitive ring + PSoC on a flex, tail on the left edge; the frame has a window for it. The five buttons are tact switches on the logic board (finding 12).
6. **The logic board is only ≈ 55 × 55 mm** (board out, 16:25): it occupies the lower half of the frame under the click wheel; the LCD and its backplate fill the upper half with nothing behind them. Usable PCB area is therefore ~30 cm², about a third of the 95 × 55 mm figure in the open-source notes — this supersedes that figure. Outline and screw positions to be measured with calipers.
7. **Board layout** (component side up, dock at the bottom): top-left small connector = headphone/hold flex; top-centre Apple-marked QFN = PMU; top-centre-right wide ZIF = LCD; top-right fine-pitch FPC connector = click wheel; two BGAs = S5L8702 + SDRAM; 12.000 MHz crystal bottom-left; bottom = 30-pin dock and HDD ZIF; bottom-right = charger/buck (4R7 inductor) and the battery connector.
8. **Click wheel flex** is Apple assembly **631-0457** (Foxconn). The finger block measures ≈ 4 mm against the ruler (16:31) → **0.3 mm pitch, 13 positions** (0.4 mm would be 5.2 mm, 0.5 mm 6.5 mm), matching the 5G connector Rockbox documents; the 5G pin assignment is the starting point for probing. Fingers are on one face only — confirm top- vs bottom-contact before choosing the board connector (HDGC 0.3K-HX-13PWB, LCSC C2919524, or Hirose/Molex equivalent). Board-side socket: 13-position 0.3 mm FPC connector with a flip latch.
9. **Frame datum (stripped frame, 16:32):** two **locating pins** at the bottom corners of the ribbed section either side of the dock cut-out pass through the two holes flanking the dock on the board — they fix the board's XY position and are the datum for the new board outline. Two **screw holes** sit on the horizontal rib about a third of the way up. The board sits behind the grid with its **component side facing the ribs**: tall parts live in the pockets, and the wheel-facing side must be essentially flat because the wheel sub-board lies against it through the frame window. Pockets as cast: left and right columns ~10–11 mm wide, centre ~23 mm, split by the rib at a third and a second rib across the centre column. The adhesive strip on the LCD divider is where the panel sticks.
10. **Volumes (Stuart, 16:57):** donor battery pocket in the back shell **45 × 20 × 5 mm** (width to double-check against photo 05); HDD **70 × 55 × 5 mm** in 1 mm rubber bumpers → **70 × 55 × 7 mm free cavity** over the ribs on the board side, overlapping the LCD area. Consequences: tall parts live in the pockets below the rib tops, so the 7 mm cavity is spare → a 505060 (≈2,500 mAh, 5 mm) or 605060 (≈3,000 mAh, 6 mm) LiPo fits there with foam margins, ~5× the donor capacity (~30 h card-to-BT at ~100 mA). The freed 45 × 20 × 5 mm shell pocket suits an X-axis LRA / iPhone-class Taptic Engine (35 × 11 × 4.5 mm).
11. **Logic board, component side, clean (17:08):** CPU **337S3520** (S5L8702, "ARM", date 0836), PMU **338S0445** (Apple-marked PCF50635), SDRAM between them, 12.000 MHz crystal. Holes: two screw holes mid-board on the rib line, two more at top-left/top-right (check against frame bosses), locating-pin notches at the bottom corners, a tab on the top-edge centre.
12. **Logic board, wheel-facing side (17:09) — the five buttons are here.** Five 6 × 6 mm SMD tactile switches sit inside an adhesive ring (≈ 37 mm diameter) where the wheel assembly was stuck: one at the centre, four on the N/S/E/W axes at ≈ **13.5 mm radius** (from the photo; to caliper). Two small holes ≈ 4.5 mm either side of the centre switch locate the wheel. The wheel assembly 821-0545-A is therefore only the capacitive ring + PSoC on a flex; the buttons are pressed through it onto the board. This explains the 13-pin flex: power, ground, serial lines and (probably) the five switch lines to the wheel's PSoC, which is why Rockbox reads button bits inside the wheel's frames — confirm with the analyser whether the switches go through the flex or straight to the SoC. Consequences for the new board: five tact switches at these exact centres and height, two locating holes, a clear 37 mm circle on that face with nothing taller than the switches; the rest of that face carries low-profile parts only (the donor has 0402s, a QFN behind the wheel connector and a via row behind the LCD ZIF). (Photo 09 earlier, captioned as the wheel sub-board rear, is in fact this face of the logic board seen through the frame.)
## Measurements still needed (before/with the scan)

- Locating-pin diameter and pin-to-pin centre distance (first dimension of the outline); board outline (≈ 55 × 55 mm) and the two screw holes relative to the pins; rib positions and height above the board; board thickness; pocket dimensions.
- Wheel flex: contact face (top/bottom) and exact finger count under a loupe.
- Tact switches: body height, exact centres (radius), the two wheel-locating hole diameters and spacing, ring-to-board gap.
- LCD ZIF and wheel ZIF pitch and pin count (count conductors over a known length with a loupe).
- Headphone/hold flex connector pitch and pin count; battery connector type.
- Screw positions (board-to-frame, frame-to-shell).
