# ADR-0020: Hold, the lock that replaces the iPod's slide switch

Status: **accepted** (owner decisions 12 Nov 2026)

## Context

A pocket player gets pressed by its own pocket. The iPod answered that with a slide switch ("Hold") that made the wheel and buttons do
nothing. neuPod has to answer it on every platform it runs on:

* the **Y2 testbed** has a Power key on its side and no slide switch;
* the **Pico 2 W bench rig** has neither (UART console keys only);
* the **final board** is the iPod 6G donor, which has the real slide switch.

Until now the Y2 had its own, partial answer inside the HAL: a short Power press put the screen to sleep (input swallowed except Power and
Centre/Menu), a 2 s hold shut the player down. The screen dimmed after an idle time (UI-5, first half) but never went fully off.

## Decision

**One Hold state, set from several places, enforced in one place (the UI frame).**

* **Where it is enforced.** `ui_frame_input()` (`app/ui/ui_frame.c`). While Hold is on, wheel turns and button presses and releases are
  swallowed before any screen sees them: they cannot act (Play/Pause included), cannot click, and cannot move anything. They only
  restart the idle clock (the screen wakes) and show a reminder ("Locked, hold Power to unlock"). Headset (AVRCP) keys do not go through
  the UI and keep working: they are deliberate. The Y2's side **volume keys** are hardware keys like the rest and are locked too
  (`ui_locked()`, checked where the Y2 handles them). Hold is RAM only: it is not saved over a power-off (a boot starts unlocked unless the
  switch says otherwise).
* **Auto-lock.** When the screen goes off (below), Hold turns on by itself. Settings > Display > **Auto-lock** (On by default, saved)
  turns that off; Hold is then only set by hand.
* **Screen off (UI-5, second stage).** After the dim (Settings > Display > Auto-dim / Dim after, unchanged) the backlight goes fully off
  after **Screen off after** (default **3 min after the dim**; Never, 15 s, 30 s, 1, 2, 3, 5 min; counted from the last input when Auto-dim is
  off; saved). The input that wakes a screen that was off is **not delivered** to the screen, locked or not. A short Power press
  (released within 0.4 s) also turns the screen off at once, and the next input wakes it. The panel is not put to sleep: only the
  backlight goes (`hal_display_backlight(0)`), as before on the Y2.
* **The Power key.** Reported by the HAL as a level (`hal_input_power_key()`), turned into `INPUT_POWER_DOWN` / `_UP` events by the input task
  (which never loses an edge: a lost release would count as a 10 s hold), timed by the UI (`app/ui/ui_hold.c`, unit tested):

  | Held | What happens |
  | --- | --- |
  | under 0.4 s (a tap) | screen off now (and Hold on, with Auto-lock) |
  | 0.4 s to 2 s | a line under the status bar fills and a pill says "Keep holding to lock" / "... to unlock"; releasing early does nothing |
  | 2 s, still held | **Hold toggles**: a confirmation ("Locked" / "Unlocked"), a haptic click where fitted, the lock icon in the status bar |
  | 5 s | still held: "Release to cancel · shutting down in 5" with a red line that grows; releasing cancels |
  | 10 s | **power-off** is requested (`hal_system_poweroff()`) |

  The power-off countdown only exists where `hal_system_can_poweroff()` is true (the Y2; the host simulator pretends). The Y2 keeps its
  contract: the request runs the firmware's orderly stop, starts `/sbin/poweroff` detached and exits with status 77 so `S91neupod-fw`
  does not respawn it. What changed is the timing (10 s, was 2 s) and who decides (the UI, not the HAL). **Charging-only mode is
  untouched:** its own loop still reboots on a 2 s hold (`y2_charge`).
* **The hardware switch.** `hal_input_hold_switch()` reports `NONE` / `OFF` / `ON`. A change becomes `INPUT_HOLD_SWITCH` and sets the
  same Hold state (on = locked, off = unlocked); a switch found on at boot locks at once. The Power gesture still toggles too, so the two
  agree after any change. Platforms without a switch report `NONE`: the Y2 and the Pico rig. The final board's GPIO is a `TODO(hw)`.
* **No Power key (Pico 2 W bench):** Hold is gesture-only there. The console key **`l`** toggles Hold (`INPUT_HOLD_TOGGLE`), and the
  reminder says "Console key l unlocks" instead of "hold Power 2 s to unlock". The bench rig cannot power itself off, so no countdown.
* **Host simulator:** **F1** is the Power key (hold it), and tour tokens `pwr:down`, `pwr:up`, `sleep:MS` and `sw:on` / `sw:off` drive the
  Power key and the switch from scripts.

## Consequences

* **RAM budget:** about 100 B of statics (Hold state, the pump state, the overlay's object pointers); the overlay's four LVGL objects exist
  only while something is shown (a few hundred bytes of the LVGL pool, freed after). The input queue grows from 16 to 64 events
  (+384 B, see UI-6 in [Runtime](../runtime.md#inter-task-data-core-0)). Measured sizes are in the pull request.
* **Flash:** three glyphs (lock, unlock, power) in the three UI fonts, the Hold logic and the Licences data (see the pull request for the
  figures).
* **Y2 behaviour change:** the HAL no longer sleeps the screen or swallows input by itself, and a 2 s hold no longer powers off (it toggles
  Hold; 10 s powers off). `docs/y2.md` describes the new behaviour.
* **A press that wakes the screen is lost on purpose,** locked or not: that is the price of a pocket-proof player.
* **A button held down when Hold comes on** (a fast-forward in progress) still gets its release: the frame remembers which presses a
  screen saw and delivers exactly those releases while locked, so nothing is left pressed.
* Tests: `ui_hold` (the timing model), `ui_frame_keys` (what the frame swallows and when), `ui_logic` (the off stage), `input_events`
  (the hand-over and the console key), `y2_input`, and the `hold_tour` / `hires_hold_tour` runs (ring, lock, swallowed keys, countdown,
  simulated power-off, switch, screen off, wake, unlock, the new Display rows) with the layout audit at 320x240 and 480x360.

## Amendment 12 November 2026: the visuals are the lock pill and the Power-hold line

The overlay box of this ADR (a boxed headline, a second line and a progress bar over the middle of the content) is replaced by the design review's HL-1 ([UI design review](../design/2026-11-12-ui-review/README.md), section 3.8):
a dark pill at the bottom of the screen with one line of text, and a 3 px line under the status bar (accent while a Power press fills towards the toggle, red for the power-off countdown). Nothing behind them is dimmed or blended, so only the pill
and the line are repainted. The gestures, timings, the swallowed input, the switch and the power-off contract are unchanged. The texts: "Keep holding to lock · 10 s = off" (no "10 s = off" where the platform cannot power off), "Locked" / "Unlocked",
"Locked · hold Power 2 s to unlock" for a swallowed key, "Release to cancel · shutting down in N", "Powering off". The lock icon is a Lucide icon in the status bar's right-hand group. RAM: the pill, its label and the line are built once with the frame (a few hundred bytes of
the LVGL pool) instead of being built and deleted per use.
